# Product Truth Intake

Resolve the facts needed for the requested images before generation or substantial product editing. Reuse an existing brief for local revisions. Background replacement that preserves product pixels, text edits, and mechanical resizing do not require reconstructing unrelated product facts.

## Relevant fields

- Product identity/category and source controlling each requested view or detail.
- Visible silhouette, proportions, parts, included quantity, interfaces, labels, and branding.
- Variant differences and the intended real use or interaction.
- Material and finish where the operation must generate or change their appearance; use [material-fidelity.md](material-fidelity.md).
- Supported claims, required copy, and user-specified must-preserve/must-avoid details.
- Marketplace, language, role/module, target size, and export requirements where needed. Record pending targets without blocking unrelated preparation.

Use a compact table when helpful. Record each consequential decision's value, source, and status: `Confirmed`, `Inferred`, `Missing/Blocking`, or `Not applicable`. Record requested transformations separately from observed facts. The brief is ready for a step when its product-defining dependencies are supported; merely listing a blocker does not resolve it.

## Evidence and requested changes

Current user statements explicitly correcting product facts take priority, followed by current authoritative photos/drawings, packaging/manual evidence, then previously checked masters. Reuse prior confirmed information unless a material conflict appears. Style references supply presentation only unless identified as evidence of the actual product; competitor goods do not establish this product's structure, included parts, branding, or performance.

Distinguish a factual correction ("the new variant is black") from an edit request ("make it black") or a concept request. An instruction to add a claim does not substantiate that claim. Record the requested transformation; preserve the distinction between a real variant and a concept. When the request clearly concerns a concept, proceed and identify that purpose in delivery. When an unresolved distinction determines whether an image truthfully represents the real goods for sale, clarify only that distinction before dependent production.

Use the claim-evidence and omission rules in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md#product-and-claim-rules). A missing optional claim need not stop unrelated images.

## Material gaps

Extract what the supplied images and brief already establish. Consider base material, process/coating, gloss, texture direction/scale, color reference, and a representative close-up only where needed by this operation.

Ask about an unknown only when different answers would change the product region being generated or edited and the evidence cannot resolve it. Preserve visible appearance without inventing an unseen manufacturing process. For example, keeping an existing gold product unchanged in a new background does not require knowing whether its finish is plating or PVD; a factual PVD callout does.

For low-risk presentation choices, state an assumption when useful and proceed. For a product-defining gap, ask a specific question rather than requesting confirmation of the entire checklist:

> 当前资料未显示侧面纹理；这张侧视图需要该信息。请提供侧面近景或说明纹理方向，其余不依赖该视角的页面继续制作。

Retain the answer for subsequent images. Approval and waiting behavior follows [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md#execution-contract); a clarification request is not an extra aesthetic approval gate.
